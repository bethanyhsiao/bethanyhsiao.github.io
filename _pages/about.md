I'm a second-year PhD student at the [Operations, Information, & Decisions Department](https://oid.wharton.upenn.edu/) at the Wharton School. I am a member of the [Computational Social Science Lab](https://css.seas.upenn.edu/) and am advised by Professor Duncan Watts.

My research focuses on how algorithms and AI impact human-decision making, particularly in areas where biases or constrained resources are prevalent. Rather than abstracting away algorithms as purely mathematical concepts, I'm interested in how humans perceive algorithms and their implications on society.

Before Wharton, I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science at the University of Pennsylvania, where I was advised by Professor Duncan Watts and mentored by Paco Barreras and Linnea Gandhi.
